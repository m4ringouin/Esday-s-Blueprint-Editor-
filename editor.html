Copies the selected blocks to the other side of a plane, with their orientation reflected (like the game's symmetry). Blocks that are not symmetrical themselves (some slopes and corners) may look turned instead of mirrored.

Swap blocks

Stretch / shrink the ship
Works on the active grid (the one in the block list). Layers are cell numbers along the ship's own axes; the ship's span is shown below.
Axis

Number of layers
Stretch repeats the blocks of the chosen layer N more times and pushes everything behind it back. Shrink deletes N layers starting at the chosen one and pulls the rest forward. Blocks that are several cells long and cross the cut are left alone (the message tells you how many). Copies only carry type, position, orientation and paint. Undo with Ctrl+Z.
Import a 3D model (.obj / .stl)
Turns a 3D model into armor blocks. With no ship open it makes a new blueprint; with a ship open it adds a NEW grid beside it, so your ship is never touched. Pick a file, choose the size, click Import. Undo with Ctrl+Z.
